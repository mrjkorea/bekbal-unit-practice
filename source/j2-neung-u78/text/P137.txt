 Point 1 가주어 it

  교과서 문장 속 Grammar
                                    다음 괄호 안의 말을 이용하여 빈칸에 들어갈 적절한 말을 쓰시오.
  01   It is difficult

                                     a language. (learn)
  02 It is very fun
                                     on the ice. (move)
  03 It is dangerous
                                          hiking alone. (go)
  04 It is hard
                                 your explanation. (understand)
  05 It would be amazing
                                               a vacation in space. (take)
