2 as+ 형용사/부사의 원급 + as

A       다음 괄호 안에서 적절한 것을 고르시오.
        01 Her face turned (as / so] red as a tomato.
        02 He speaks Korean as [good / well] as a native.
        03 Books are not as [interesting / interestingly] as movies.
        04 The bus is [as not / not as] fast as the subway.
        05 This laptop is as [light/lighter) as a magazine.




 B 다음 두 문장을 괄호 안의 단어와 「as - as'...구문을 이용하여 한 문장으로 바꿔 쓴 문장을 완성하시오.
         01 Fast food is not healthy. Fruit and vegetables are healthy. (healthy)
                Fast food is                                           fruit and vegetables.

         02 The river is 200 meters wide. The canal is 200 meters wide, too. (wide)
             -The river is                                          the canal.

         03 The bag weighs 10 kilograms. The box weighs 10 kilograms, too. (heavy)
              → The bag is                                         the box.


          04 He ran 100 meters in 12 seconds. His friend ran 100 meters in 12 seconds, too. (fast)
                 He ran 100 meters                                            his friend.


          05 This room costs 50 dollars per night. The hotel room costs 80 dollars per night. (expensive)
              - This room is                                           the hotel room.




    C      다음 문장에서 어법상 틀린 부분을 바르게 고쳐 문장을 다시 쓰시오.
           01 This apple is as sweetness as that one.



           02 The baby is as more lovely as an angel.



           03 Today is not as cold yesterday.



           04 This game is as not fun as the old one.



            05 The water in the lake is as clearly as crystal.
